SlamVision Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 20, 2026

This policy covers the SlamVision app for Roku streaming players and Roku TVs ("the app"), published by Justin Hatten ("we", "us"). The app plays classic wrestling as live channels with a TV guide, plus an On Demand library.

Short version: no accounts, no sign-in, no ads, no tracking, and no purchases in the app. We don't collect or sell personal information.

Information we collect

We don't collect personal information through the app. We don't ask for your name, email address, phone number, location or payment details. The app contains no analytics, advertising or tracking software (SDKs).

How the app connects to the internet

To show channels and play videos, your Roku connects directly to these services. Like any internet request, each one receives standard technical details such as your IP address, the time, and a basic app identifier ("SlamVision-Roku").

  • GitHub Pages (jhatren927-ai.github.io), which hosts the channel lineup, TV guide, channel logos and On Demand catalog. GitHub's privacy statement: docs.github.com.
  • The SlamVision website (ring-channel-tv.emergent.host), which tells the app which tape is playing right now on a channel so it can join the show mid-tape. The hosting provider may keep standard server logs for security and operations. We don't use them to identify you.
  • The Internet Archive (archive.org), which hosts the video files. Videos stream from archive.org to your Roku. See archive.org/about/terms.

Information saved on your Roku

The app saves a few small settings on your Roku only: the last channel you watched (so it opens there next time), and your place in On Demand tapes (so you can resume). This information stays on your device and isn't sent to us. Removing the app from your Roku deletes it.

Roku

Roku, Inc. runs the device, the Roku Streaming Store and the Roku platform, and may collect information under its own privacy policy, which we don't control: go.roku.com/privacy. You can manage Roku privacy settings on your device or at my.roku.com.

Children

The app isn't directed to children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the effective date above.
